Slack-ready summary

Ran 250 real-data comparisons on SWE-chat + LongMemEval rendered.
- LongMemEval rendered: grep/file search 12/75 vs full context 8/75 exact.
- SWE-chat: exact scoring is invalid for long coding replies; both show 0/50 exact.
- Rough semantic SWE-chat signal: full context avg 0.196, grep avg 0.139.
- Caveats: LME is rendered/clipped browser context, not raw lossless JSON. This is not pass@2; one completion per arm.
