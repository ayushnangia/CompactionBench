CompactionBench

What happens when AI context gets compressed?

Experiment 1 — Does compression need to know the question?
BABILong 5-task test (qa1, qa2, qa5, qa11, qa14). Gold answers are single words hidden in ~130k tokens of noise. Compressed to ~150 tokens before asking.
Conditionqa1qa2qa5qa11qa14Total
Raw (no compression) ✗✗✓✓✓ 3/5
Compressed (knows the question) ✓✗✓✗✓ 3/5
Compressed (does NOT know question) ✗✗✗✗✗ 0/5
Takeaway: Compression with the question = matches normal performance. Without the question = completely blind. The compressor kept the wrong facts every single time when it did not know what to look for.
Experiment 2 — Can a smarter model recover lost information?
15 scattered "job_failed" events hidden in ~158k tokens of filler. Task: count them.

gpt-5.4-miniRaw context9, 13, 14, 10, 12

Takeaway: GPT-5.4 counts perfectly with full context. After compression, it fails completely — guessing 3 instead of 15. A stronger model cannot recover what the compressor deleted. Information loss is final.
